## Findings

### No crashes, no hangs, no sanitizer (ASan) reports on any target

Every target exited cleanly with `DONE` after consuming its full time budget. No `ERROR:` / `SUMMARY:` blocks, no `deadly signal`, no ASan reports in any of the ten logs.

### Informational (non-crash) observations

1. **Two slow units (~11 s single-execution each), within -timeout=25**
   - `authenticated_vault`: `fuzz/artifacts/authenticated_vault/slow-unit-c4624d19be8380c6dff51704f2316ec8c664aebc` (120 bytes)
   - `migrate_legacy`: `fuzz/artifacts/migrate_legacy/slow-unit-df38bcea04516b97e16c9ae5b0f249159c0186a9` (197 bytes)
   - Both reproduced post-campaign at ~11 s per single execution (libFuzzer's 10-s slow-unit threshold), then exited cleanly. This is the expected KDF-policy gate behavior under the harness-only `fuzz-fast-kdf` feature (8 MiB/t=1 floor with ASan overhead), the same informational class as the 0.4.0 campaign's migrate_legacy slow unit. Both artifacts are committed. **Not defects. No action needed.**
2. **Corpus churn**: one `import_bitwarden` seed (`fa281bfe…`) was REDUCEd into a smaller equivalent (`14ae5214…`) and recorded as a rename; expected libFuzzer corpus minimization, committed.

## Verdict

**Campaign clean.** Zero crashes, hangs, OOMs, or sanitizer findings across 10 targets / 21,600s active fuzz / ~678.5M executions at commit 524c6dff. The only artifacts produced (two ~11-s slow units) are expected KDF-policy behavior within bounds and require no source change. latchkey 0.4.1 passes the fuzz release gate from this campaign's evidence.

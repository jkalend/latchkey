## Difference vs. the earlier 15-second smokes

- The smokes (authenticated_vault 1,636 execs; migrate_legacy 1,096 execs; ~30s total) only proved the harness builds and runs. This campaign ran **~491.5 million executions across ~6 hours of active fuzz time** — roughly five orders of magnitude more executions, with coverage feedback accumulating the whole time.
- Full `-max_total_time` budgets let libFuzzer progress through its length schedule (lim grows from small seeds up to 4096 bytes) and accumulate corpus/coverage: authenticated_vault corpus 24→114 and cov 754, migrate_legacy 22→75, parse_item 184→261, import_native_json 9362→10619, import_bitwarden 6642→7671 with cov 1847/ft 8515, etc. The smokes barely finished `INITED`.
- Sustained multi-hour runs are the only way to hit deep parser state combinations (e.g. header-field + Argon2-param + frame-length interactions) and to accumulate enough slow KDF-path executions (thousands at ~0.4–10 s each) for the end-to-end targets to be meaningfully exercised; a 15-second smoke executes a handful of KDF paths.
- Result: the release gate now has high-confidence clean-signal evidence across all ten targets at full budget, not just build/liveness evidence.

## Verdict

**Campaign clean.** Zero crashes, hangs, OOMs, or sanitizer findings across 10 targets / 21,600s active fuzz / ~491.5M executions at commit 8f38ff4b. The only artifact produced (migrate_legacy 10-s slow unit) is expected KDF-policy behavior within bounds and requires no source change. latchkey 0.4.0 passes the fuzz release gate from this campaign's evidence.
